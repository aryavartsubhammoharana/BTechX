Biology & Environmental Studies

Biological concepts, ecology, pollution control, and environmental management (CY202).

1

Cells and Biomolecules - Structure

Cell architecture, organelle structure, and molecular composition of biomolecules (proteins, carbohydrates, lipids, nucleic acids).

Download Notes QNA
2

Cells and Biomolecules - Function

Biochemical functions of biomolecules, enzyme kinetics, cellular transport, metabolic pathways, and energy transduction.

Download Notes QNA
3

Structure and Function of Ecosystem

Ecosystem components, trophic levels, energy flow models, food chains & webs, biogeochemical cycles, and ecological stability.

Download Notes QNA
4

Air and Noise Pollution: Source, cause and treatment

Air & noise pollution sources, causes, particulate & gaseous emission control devices, noise standards, and abatement techniques.

Download Notes QNA
5

Water pollution abatement and Solid waste management

Water quality parameters, wastewater treatment methods (primary, secondary, tertiary), solid waste classification, recovery, recycling, and disposal.
