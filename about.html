Our Purpose

BTechX is a dedicated academic resource platform built to serve B.Tech students. DLM is made by Developer. Our core purpose is to provide a centralized, free, and highly organized hub for engineering notes, lecture PDFs, and official syllabi.

By removing paywalls and login barriers, our goal is to ensure that every student has uninterrupted access to high-quality study materials, perfectly aligned with the university's curriculum.
