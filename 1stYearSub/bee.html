AI Summary: Basic Electrical Engineering provides a comprehensive introduction to DC and AC circuit analysis, transformers, and rotating electrical machines. It is the foundational course for understanding power distribution and electro-mechanical energy conversion.

1

DC Circuit Analysis

Ohm's law, Kirchhoff's laws, mesh and node analysis, network theorems, and DC circuit applications.

2

AC Circuit Analysis

Phasors, impedance, resonance, three-phase circuits, and power factor correction.

3

Transformers

Principle of transformer, equivalent circuit, losses, efficiency, regulation, and testing.

4

DC Machines

Construction, EMF equation, armature reaction, commutation, characteristics, and speed control of DC machines.

5

AC Machines

Alternator, synchronous motor, three-phase induction motor, single-phase motors.
